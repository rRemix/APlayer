<p><i>APlayer</i> is a beautiful and powerful music player. Next to other useful features you would expect from a musicplayer, it offers a.o.:</p><ul><li>Configurable tabs: songs, artists, albums, folders, playlists, remote (WebDAV)</li><li>Local and online lyrics: embedded/local/online, word-by-word, searchable with priority</li><li>Floating lyrics and home screen widgets</li><li>Themes: light, dark, and AMOLED black, with customizable colors</li><li>Auto download album and artist artwork</li><li>Built-in tag editor for title/artist/album/lyrics</li><li>Playlists: create, edit, import, export, and per-playlist sorting</li><li>WebDAV streaming from your personal cloud storage</li><li>Playback controls: equalizer, speed control, sleep timer</li><li>Lock screen controls and Android media notification</li><li>Bluetooth/wired headset media buttons</li><li>Auto scan media library or manual folder scan</li></ul>
